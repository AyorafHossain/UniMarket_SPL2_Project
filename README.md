UniMarket – University Student Marketplace
About the Project
UniMarket is a university-focused marketplace application .The main goal of UniMarket is to provide a dedicated platform where university students can buy, sell, exchange, and rent academic and other useful items within the student community.
The project consists of two applications:
•	 User Application – Used by students to browse, buy, sell, exchange, and rent products.
•	 Admin Panel – Used by administrators to manage users, products, orders, and support activities.

Project Objectives
The major objectives of UniMarket are:
•	Provide a student-focused marketplace for university communities.
•	Make buying and selling used academic items easier.
•	Allow students to exchange or rent products.
•	Provide communication between buyers and sellers.
•	Provide administrators with tools to manage the marketplace.
•	Create a convenient and organized alternative to informal student-to-student buying and selling.

Key Features
User Application
•	Buy & Sell – Students can browse products and list their own products for sale.
•	Book Exchange – Students can exchange academic books and other study materials.
•	Product Exchange – Users can request exchanges between products.
•	Rent Items – Students can rent useful products for a specific duration.
•	 Wishlist – Save products for later.
•	Cart & Checkout – Add products to cart and proceed with orders.
•	Real-Time Chat – Communication between buyers and sellers.
•	Push Notifications – Notifications for orders, messages, and other activities.
•	 Support Tickets – Users can submit support requests.
•	Rating & Review – Users can provide ratings and reviews.
•	 Order Management – Track and manage product orders.
•	 Negotiable Price / Make Offer – Buyers can communicate with sellers regarding price.
Admin Panel
•	User Management – View and manage registered users.
•	Product Management – Monitor listed products.
•	Order Management – Monitor marketplace transactions.
•	Support Ticket Management – Review and resolve user support requests.
•	Analytics Dashboard – View marketplace statistics and activity.
•	User Control – Manage or restrict problematic accounts when necessary.
